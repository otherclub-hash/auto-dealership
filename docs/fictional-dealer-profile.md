# Fictional Dealer Profile: Northwind Motors of Birchwood Bay

Purpose: stand in the dealer's shoes. This is the demo tenant, and also a realistic model of the business we are selling to, so every feature can be judged by what it does to this dealer's numbers.

Everything here is fictional. All figures are plausible planning assumptions for a mid-size suburban franchise store, to be replaced with real dealer data when we have it. Brand and model names must be trademark-screened before public use.

---

## 1. The fictional carmaker: Northwind Motor Company (NMC)

### 2.4 Lead funnel today (internet leads, per month)

| Step | Count | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| Web and third-party leads | 450 | |
| Contacted within 5 minutes | 90 | 20% |
| Appointments set | 112 | 25% of leads |
| Appointments that show | 62 | 55% |
| Sales from internet leads | 25 | 40% of shows |

**Opportunity:** raising 5-minute response to 70%, show rate to 65% (confirmations and reminders), and lead-to-appointment to 32% moves roughly 25 sales to about 40 per month. At about $2,300 combined front and back gross per unit, that is on the order of $30,000 per month in added gross, before service. These are assumptions to validate, but they show the shape of the pitch.

### 2.5 Service funnel today

- About 70 ROs per day, 5 days a week, plus Saturday.
- Phone-heavy: advisors spend a large share of the day answering "how much for X" calls and scheduling.
- Price-shopping drop-off: callers who get a range or "it depends" often do not book.
- Declined services (brakes, tires, filters) are rarely followed up.
- Retention falls off after the warranty period.

## 3. A realistic service menu with firm pricing (flagship example)

Oil change on the **Ridgeback**, priced from the vehicle, not from a guess.

Pricing formula: labor + (quarts x price per quart) + filter + shop and disposal fee.

| Engine | Oil spec | Capacity (qt) | Labor | Oil ($9.50/qt synthetic) | Filter | Fees | **Firm price** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2.7L turbo I4 | 0W-20 | 6.0 | $39.00 | $57.00 | $14.00 | $3.50 | **$113.50** |
| 3.6L V6 | 0W-20 | 6.5 | $39.00 | $61.75 | $14.00 | $3.50 | **$118.25** |
| 5.3L V8 | 0W-20 | 8.0 | $39.00 | $76.00 | $16.00 | $3.50 | **$134.50** |
| 3.3L diesel | 5W-40 | 7.5 | $49.00 | $93.75 | $29.00 | $3.50 | **$175.25** |

Policy to show: "Your price is $134.50. If the engine needs more than 8.0 quarts, you will never pay more than 8.5 quarts' worth ($138.75); we will text you before doing anything beyond that." The tool also shows the old problem solved: no "anywhere up to $189" answer ever has to be given. (All capacities and prices fictional.)

Other menu items to seed: tire rotation, multi-point inspection, cabin and engine air filter, battery test and replace, brake pads and rotors (by axle), alignment, wiper blades, seasonal tire changeover, state inspection-equivalent items, fluid services, recall repair (free, scheduling only).

## 5. Real-world conditions the demo must respect

- Inventory changes daily: vehicles sell, arrive, are in transit or on hold. The demo should simulate a feed with sold and "arriving soon" states.
- Pricing shows MSRP, dealer discount, manufacturer incentives with eligibility, and a transparent fee line (doc fee, taxes, title and registration). The state doc fee and tax rules need verification before any real dealer uses it.
- Incentives vary by ZIP, credit tier, and expire monthly.
- Used vehicles need history report links and inspection details.
- Credit applications are regulated (privacy, adverse action rules); the demo uses a mock vendor and stores no SSN.
- Customer consent is required for text and email marketing.
- Phone and walk-in traffic persists alongside digital; click-to-call must feed the same lead inbox.
- Staff turnover is high; the admin must be simple enough for a new BDC hire in a day.
- Weekends and evenings are when shoppers browse; after-hours response needs an answer (auto-reply plus next-morning task).

## 6. Day in the life: scenarios to script in the demo

1. **Sunday 9 p.m.:** a shopper searches for a Boundary with a third row under $55,000 and saves two. Instant text confirms, and the BDC queue shows it Monday 8 a.m. with what they viewed.
2. **Trade-in:** a Tamarack owner enters their plate and answers five condition cards, gets a range, books an inspection; the used manager sees a hot acquisition lead.
3. **The oil change:** a Ridgeback owner selects the engine and sees $134.50, books a Saturday slot, gets a reminder; the service director sees a filled bay.
4. **Declined brakes:** an owner who declined brakes last month gets a text with a price and booking link.
5. **Parts:** a local shop logs in, finds fitment by VIN, and reorders a brake kit.
6. **Manager view:** Monday morning dashboard: leads, response time, appointments, shows, service bookings, aged inventory, revenue attributed to the site.
7. **A new dealer, in a day:** the same app loads as a different fictional dealer with new colors and inventory, to prove the template.

## 7. How this feeds the build

- Demo tenant data: NMC lineup, about 330 vehicles (new, used, CPO) across two locations, service menu with capacity tables, 300 to 500 parts, accessories and bundles, specials, staff, and reviews.
- Dealer dashboard metrics use the funnel numbers in section 2 as the starting point, so the "before and after" is believable.
- Pricing conversations use sections 2.2 to 2.4: what the dealer spends today and what a funnel improvement is worth.
- Everything is stored as tenant config and seed data, never hard-coded, so swapping in a real dealer later is a config change.
