# Evidence for the dealer problems (first pass, Oct 30 2026)

Source quality: mostly trade press and vendor-published studies. Treat as directional; verify primary sources (NADA Data, Cox Automotive studies) before quoting to a dealer.

## 1. Service and fixed ops are a large share of profit, and dealers are losing service visits

- Fixed operations (service, parts, body) have been reported at roughly 46% to 53% of total dealership gross profit in NADA-based figures, on about 12% to 13% of sales dollars. The share dips when new/used vehicle margins surge (36.5% in the first ten months of 2021). Sources: [Mercer Capital](https://mercercapital.com/dont-turn-a-blind-eye-to-fixed-operations), [Ventavid glossary citing NADA 2025](https://www.ventavid.com/glossary/fixed-operations).
- Fixed ops gross margins are cited around 45% to 50% (same sources).
- Cox Automotive's 2025 study: dealerships have lost about 12% of service visits to competition since 2018; dealers capture about 29% of service visits. Sources: [Cox Automotive](https://www.coxautoinc.com/insights/new-cox-automotive-study-finds-dealerships-have-lost-12-of-service-visits-to-competition-since-2018/), [Digital Dealer](https://digitaldealer.com/news/dealerships-lose-12-of-service-visits-since-2018-cox-automotive-study-finds/168288/).
- Older Cox findings: about 70% of buyers did not return to the selling dealer for service in the past year (2018 study); about 71% defect by year five (2021, cited by [KBB](https://b2b.kbb.com/resources/revenue-importance-service-after-sale)). Different years and definitions, not directly comparable.
- Service customers are more likely to buy their next vehicle from the same dealer (74% likely, cited via secondary sources).

## 2. Internet lead response is slow

- Pied Piper 2024 mystery shop of 4,000+ franchise stores: median response to an internet lead during business hours was 47 minutes (reported via vendor roundup, see [Webfones](https://auto.webfones.com/bdc/dealership-lead-response-time)).
- Older Digital Air Strike study of 1,500 dealerships: only 16% replied within 15 minutes, 18% never replied, 36% answered after-hours requests. See [Auto Remarketing](https://autoremarketing.com/trends/study-dealership-online-lead-response-time-often-falls-short) and [Wards](https://wardsauto.com/dealers/study-says-dealers-slow-to-respond).
- A 2026 mystery shop of 53 stores (Clearline) reported a median first response of 11.5 hours, a small sample that likely reflects methodology differences ([BDC.ai](https://www.bdc.ai/research/speed-to-lead-dealership-ai-2026)).
- Conversion lift claims for fast response (doubling within 20 minutes, large lift within 60 seconds) come from vendors and cross-industry studies; treat as directional.
- Gap: no independent source for "percent of leads never contacted" beyond the older study.

## 3. Used inventory sourcing

- Trade-ins and customer-sourced cars are generally cheaper to acquire than auction cars. One dealer survey cited a gap of about $1,500 per unit, but few dealers track it; another vendor analysis reports higher retail gross on cars sourced from a dealer's own database. Sources: [vAuto](https://www.vauto.com/learning-center/blog-dale-pollak-all/a-pay-up-pitfall-as-dealers-source-customer-vehicles), [Dealership Guy](https://news.dealershipguy.com/p/dealers-overlook-profitable-used-inventory-sitting-in-their-own-customer-databases).
- Auction cars make up 30%+ of many dealers' used inventory ([Cox Automotive](https://www.coxautoinc.com/insights/4-ways-to-turn-auction-cars-into-advantage-at-your-store/)).
- Gap: no clean figure for the trade-in share of used inventory.

## What this means for the plan

- Strongest evidence: **service retention and fixed ops value**, and **slow lead response**.
- Weakest evidence: **used inventory sourcing** (directionally supported, numbers vendor-sourced).
- Our fictional dealer's numbers in `fictional-dealer-profile.md` are planning assumptions consistent with, but not taken from, these sources. A real dealer's data replaces them.
